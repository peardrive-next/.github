This is where the PearDrive team moves fast. Ideas get tested, tools get built, and prototypes take shape here before anything reaches our open-source packages. Released and supported code lives in the public [**peardrive**](https://github.com/peardrive) organization.

Expect things to be rough around the edges. Repos may be unfinished, change without notice, or be retired once they've served their purpose. When a prototype proves itself, it graduates to the public org.

> [!NOTE]
> Nothing in this organization is part of the official PearDrive release or covered by its support.
